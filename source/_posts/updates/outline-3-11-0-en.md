This update changes Free export credits and outline navigation access, alongside navigation stability and interface improvements.

## Free export credits

Free users share **3 advanced HTML or PDF exports per week**. Both formats use the same allowance, which **resets every Monday**.

For example, exporting one HTML file and one PDF leaves one export for the week. Markdown exports do not use these credits. PRO and active PRO trials include unlimited HTML/PDF exports.

## The first 100 outline entries

Free users can navigate **the first 100 outline entries in each conversation**. PRO or an active PRO trial unlocks navigation to entry 101 and beyond.

This controls outline navigation access; it does not delete or truncate your original conversation. Outline search, bookmarks, highlights and notes remain available.

## Navigation stability

Improved navigation to older content in long conversations, including stability during repeated navigation. Older messages may still load progressively, and wait times depend on the host website and network conditions.

## Interface improvements

Refined selected controls, light/dark styling and the account benefits panel. The quick menu and avatar entry now present account information consistently, including remaining exports and outline navigation access.

## Check your installed version

Open the OutlineSave browser toolbar popup to check your installed version and account benefits. Store rollouts may arrive at different times; available features follow your installed version.
